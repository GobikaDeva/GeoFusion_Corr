3. Does C1's completeness gain survive a looser or stricter fusion? (reduced fusion sweep)

Both models' depth maps (seed 2 each, already dumped at 1600×1152) are re-fused and scored with the official metric. The full 32-setting grid was too slow (~3.3–4 h per model-scan), so it was stopped and replaced by a reduced sweep.

Rule, fixed before any result was looked at: all 22 scans; family geo (world-distance check) at distance 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 mm; consistent-view count fixed at the official 4; prob 0.3. That is the official setting (0.2 mm), one stricter and two looser. Estimated ~30–35 min per model-scan, 38 to run.

What this sweep can support: whether C1's completeness gain and accuracy loss over A0f depend on the fusion distance threshold, and a matched-point-count comparison along that one axis, for scans whose point counts overlap.
What it cannot support: any conclusion about the view-count threshold (2/3/6 views not run); any conclusion about MVSNet-style reprojection/relative-depth filters (not run); anything about the gap to CL-MVSNet (different checkpoint and resolution, not part of this sweep). It is single-seed (seed 2 of each model).

Matched setup (checked against configs and logs):

  • Both runs are initialised from runs/B2_nogeo_finetune/seed2/ckpt_final.pt (the "initialized weights from" line is in both train.log files). Everything upstream of the final stage is the same checkpoint.
  • Same final-stage budget: 1500 steps each, lr 5e-4, same data, batch and loss settings. The config diff (B2_nogeo.yaml vs B2_mono_feat.yaml) shows only the mono-feature input (frozen Depth-Anything-V2-Small, zero-initialised injection at coarse and mid), data.geometry_prior (mono_sparse vs none) and use_geo_term (true vs false).
  • L_geo is off in both: geo_weight 0.0 in both; the control has use_geo_term true with weight 0. B2 never reads the prior input, so the geometry_prior and use_geo_term differences do not change the computation.
  • One budget difference: mono_feat has 12,480 more trainable parameters (2,133,939 vs 2,121,459, the injection layers), plus the frozen Depth-Anything model. Step time is about the same (4.17 vs 4.21 s/step).

Result:

  • ΔOverall −0.0127 (mono_feat − control), larger than 0.009.
  • Better on only 11 of 22 scans.
  • scan48 alone accounts for −0.155 (0.870 → 0.715). Without scan48, the mean difference over the other 21 scans is −0.006, below 0.009.
  • Single seed.
  • The gain is from completeness (−0.029); accuracy is slightly worse (+0.003).

We read this as a screening signal concentrated on the ambiguous scan, not a confirmed gain. A second seed would be needed before Track 1 counts as a result.
