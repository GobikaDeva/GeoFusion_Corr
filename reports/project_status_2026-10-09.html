GeoFusionNet / GeoCorr: Project Status Report

Status as of 2026-10-09, 19:20 UTC · main @ e92a340 · Prepared for Gobika S
Summary. The frozen Stage 0 baseline (A0f) scores 0.491 ± 0.009 overall on full-res DTU (3 seeds × 22 scans). The geometric prior does not help in any of the ways tested: as an input (B1), as a loss (L_geo), or as a confidence-gated replacement. In every case the effect is within noise or limited to a few outlier scans. Only one change has clearly helped so far: more cost-volume capacity (B2). It reaches 0.414 / 0.418 on two seeds, against A0f's 0.479 on seed 2. The gain comes from completeness, and per-view depth metrics did not improve. The third B2 seed, the B2 no-L_geo ablation and a frozen mono-feature variant are running or queued now.

3. How we got here

3.1 Fixing the pipeline (2026-09-23 to 09-25)

  • Constant-depth failure: early A0 runs had ~67 mm error. The cause was edge-aware smoothness applied to raw mm depth. Normalizing by per-image mean depth fixed it. A wrong score sign was ruled out.
  • Evaluation: evaluation before 2026-09-23 had no fusion filtering and used a non-official metric. It now uses GeoMVSNet's open3d fusion (prob 0.3, 0.2 mm, 4 views) and the official DTUeval protocol, cross-checked against the reference. Full resolution is 1600×1152. Numbers from before the fix are not comparable.
  • Undertraining: A0 saw ~40k samples, about 9% of the reference recipe. A CL-MVSNet-style LR fine-tune helped only ~5% (val fine median 1.75 → 1.65–1.70 mm), so the LR schedule does not explain the plateau.
  • Cost-volume fixes: out-of-view masking plus trilinear regularizer upsampling cut sawtooth from 41% to 0.1%. They are now the default.

3.2 Failure analysis: scan48 and the outlier scans (11, 13, 48, 62, 77)

  • The main failure is photometric ambiguity. On wrong pixels, GT is the global cost minimum on only 30%, a secondary minimum on 41%, and no minimum on 28%. Our photometric loss itself prefers the wrong depth.
  • Robust losses (L0.5, min-view) and best-k view aggregation did not fix it. Widening the depth windows at test time made it worse.
  • The CL-MVSNet consistency loss (ICC + SCC, ported with ×0.0163 weight calibration) was within noise on aggregate val. On scan48 it was worse at the final checkpoint (4.34 vs 3.45 mm). Dropped; A0f (fixes only) was frozen as Stage 0.

3.4 Capacity: B2_capacity

  • Changes: FPN 32/96/192, 8-group correlation cost, 3D UNet regularizer. 2.03M params vs A0f's 121k. Same 20k + 6k + 1.5k training chain as A0f.
  • Fused point clouds are much better (comp 0.36 vs 0.52; scan48 1.213 → 0.708). Per-view depth metrics are worse, though: val fine median 1.76 vs 1.52 mm, and scan48 fine median 3.96 vs 3.45 mm. Competing dips on scan48 fell from 18.75 to 12.90%.
  • Fusion pass-through check: the confidence filter is close to a no-op, and the point counts are about the same as A0f's (22.3M vs 22.1M). At a pass rate matched to A0f, B2 still scores 0.416. So the gain comes from where the points land, not how many survive fusion. The exact mechanism has not been verified.

4. Open questions and next decisions

  • Is B2's gain robust? Two of three seeds agree (0.414, 0.418), and the seed 1 result is due ~10-11. The decision on the 3-seed mean ± std comes from aggregate_b2_3seed.sh.
  • Does L_geo matter on B2? Answered by the B2_nogeo seed 2 vs B2 seed 2 matched pair.
  • Can monocular information help without the noisy metric prior? Answered by B2_mono_feat (frozen DA-V2-S features as a zero-init residual) vs B2_nogeo seed 2.
  • GeoCorr Lite decision (still open): the evidence so far does not support the prior as the paper's main contribution. Capacity and monocular features are the remaining candidates.
  • Why B2 fuses better despite worse per-view depth is not yet explained.
